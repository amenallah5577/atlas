ECHOSPHERE ATLAS

Terms and Conditions

Atlas provides conversation, document analysis, and optional web research. These Terms and Conditions explain how to use the service responsibly.

Acceptable use

  • Use Atlas lawfully and respect other people's privacy and rights.
  • Do not use the service for fraud, harassment, exploitation, or instructions intended to harm others.
  • Do not attempt to access another account, bypass usage limits, steal credentials, or disrupt the service.
  • Do not upload documents or personal information that you do not have permission to use.

Saved information

Account information and saved conversations are stored so you can sign in and return to your history. Passwords are stored as hashes. Original uploaded PDFs are processed in memory. Saved answers can contain excerpts and information derived from those PDFs.

If you choose social sign-in, Atlas stores your provider's account identifier and basic profile details, including your email address. Atlas does not request access to your email inbox or store provider access or refresh tokens.

You can delete saved conversations from Atlas.

Research and limitations

Atlas can make mistakes. Check cited sources before using important factual claims, prices, vacancies, or recommendations. A retrieved page does not guarantee that an offer, price, or vacancy is still available.

Research is subject to provider availability and usage limits. The owner may pause new registrations or live web research through the administration settings.

Your account

Use a password that you do not use elsewhere, keep your sign-in details private, and sign out on shared devices. The owner administration area is reserved for the configured owner account. Creating a normal account does not grant administrative access.

You can update your display name and profile photo, export your saved conversations, manage sessions, and delete your account from Settings. Profile photos are resized and stored without their original metadata. The configured owner account cannot delete itself through these controls.

Optional parental controls use a PIN to protect browsing, PDF uploads, and explicit-content settings on this account. Content filtering is limited and may miss some material or block some legitimate requests. Break reminders and browser notifications depend on an open Atlas tab and your browser's permissions.

Account activity and moderation

The site owner can review saved chats and activity for moderation. When you create an account, you agree that the site owner may review your saved prompts, responses, research results, and related evidence to administer Atlas and check compliance with these terms.

Activity logs retain submitted requests and review notes after conversation deletion so the owner can investigate abuse. The owner administration page provides access to saved account history and these activity records.

The owner may disable an account after reviewing activity that violates these rules. Review decisions are made by the owner. An account's content is not automatically treated as a violation merely because a search contains a sensitive topic.
